# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want DevCrate to be a welcoming, harassment-free space for everyone, regardless of experience level, background, identity, or how new you are to open source. Many contributors here are making their first-ever pull request — we take that seriously.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and patient, especially with first-time contributors.
- Give feedback on the code, not the person.
- Accept that maintainers and reviewers are volunteers responding when they can.
- Assume good faith; ask questions before assuming bad intent.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language or jokes in any project space (issues, PRs, discussions, commit messages).
- Publishing others' private information without consent.
- Submitting plagiarized or knowingly misrepresented work.
- Gaming project metrics (fake issues, spam PRs, review manipulation).
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces (GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions) and in public spaces when someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Violations can be reported by emailing the maintainer at **kohariaakif@gmail.com**. Reports will be reviewed and handled as discreetly as possible. Maintainers may remove comments, close PRs, or block contributors who violate this Code of Conduct, temporarily or permanently, at their discretion.
